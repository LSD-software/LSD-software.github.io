A LABORATORY.
A BROKEN MEMORY.

You wake inside a laboratory that feels familiar for reasons you cannot explain. Machines hum behind sealed doors. Notes contradict one another. Rooms trigger flashes of moments you cannot fully remember.

Explore the facility, examine environmental clues and piece together the sequence of events. Each flashback reveals another fragment — but not every memory can be trusted.
